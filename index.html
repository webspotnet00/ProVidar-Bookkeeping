Who We Are

We understand that managing financial records can be challenging, especially for business owners who already have multiple responsibilities. That's why we aim to provide practical, organized and reliable bookkeeping support that helps businesses stay financially organized.

By combining professional service, attention to detail and cloud-based bookkeeping technology, we help our clients maintain better control of their financial information.

Our Mission

We provide reliable financial records that business owners can depend on — accurate, current and understandable — so every client always knows where their business stands and can decide with confidence rather than guesswork.

Our Vision

To be the bookkeeping partner that small and growing businesses trust first, known for accuracy, honesty and service that goes beyond the ledger.

Most business owners do not come to us with an accounting question. They come with a problem that has been quietly costing them money, time or sleep. Hire ProVidar and the pain points below stop being yours to carry.

“I don't know if I'm actually making money.”

“My records are months behind.”

We clean up and catch up the backlog, then keep the books current so it never piles up twice.

“I'm scared of what the tax office will find.”

Every figure is reconciled and checked before it reaches a filing, so you file from records you trust.

“Receipts and invoices are everywhere except in a system.”

We put a simple, repeatable process in place so documents land in the right place on the day they arrive.

“I'm spending my weekends on bookkeeping instead of my business.”

Pricing

Our monthly fee is based on the number of transactions in each account. One flat monthly rate per account, agreed before any work starts — no hourly surprises, and no charge for the initial review of your books.

Monthly bookkeeping fees by transaction volume, per account
Transactions per account, per month Monthly fee
Up to 50 transactions USD 100
51–100 transactions USD 150
101–150 transactions USD 200–300 depending on categorization work

Fees are quoted per account. Extra volume, or any amount outside the scope above, is confirmed with you before the work is done — never after.

Clean-Up of Prior Periods

Quoted separately, once we have seen how far back the records go. We review the backlog first and give you a figure in writing before any catch-up work begins.

Always Included

A dedicated contact who knows your books, plain-language reports, deadlines tracked and met, confidential handling, and no lock-in — month to month.

Not Included

Tax filing and tax advice, audits and assurance work, and legal or investment advice — all referred to a licensed professional.
