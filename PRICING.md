# Pricing

Our monthly fee is based on the number of transactions in each account. One flat monthly rate per account, agreed before any work starts — no hourly surprises, no billing for a phone call.

Every plan is delivered remotely, on cloud bookkeeping, so your records are available to you at any time.

---

## Monthly fees

| Transactions per account, per month | Monthly fee |
|---|---|
| Up to 50 transactions | USD 100 |
| 51–100 transactions | USD 150 |
| 101–150 transactions | USD 200–300, depending on categorization work |

Fees are quoted per account. Extra volume or amounts outside the scope above are confirmed with you before the work is done — never after.

---

## What's covered

- Day-to-day recording of sales, purchases, expenses and payments
- Bank and account reconciliation — every account matched to the ledger
- Accounts payable and receivable tracking
- Financial statements prepared and explained in plain language
- Monthly reporting, with a short review of what the numbers are telling you

---

## Clean-up of prior periods

Quoted separately, once we have seen how far back the records go. We look at the backlog first and give you a figure in writing before any catch-up work begins.

## What's always included

- **No charge for the initial review of your books.**
- A dedicated point of contact who knows your books
- Reports explained in plain language, not accounting jargon
- Deadlines tracked and met
- Confidential handling of your records
- No lock-in — month to month

## Not included

These fall outside bookkeeping and are referred to a licensed professional:

- Tax filing and tax advice
- Audits and assurance work
- Legal or investment advice
